Career Ready

Our group of young artists and designers are part of the government-run programme called "Career Ready" This is an internship-style programme lasting four weeks, preparing young people for their future careers and the general world of work. This is an incredible opportunity for our young people and it aims in helping to level up skills and confidence as-well as experience.

Exhibition

We will be hosting an exhibition held in the Keiller Centre supported by NEoN Digital Arts and Federation Gallery on 22nd July 2023. The motive of this exhibition is to showcase our young artist Wictoria's work that she has produced at Dundee college during her HND animation course. Wictoria's animations are absolutely fantastic and are sure to catch the interest of those that enter the Keiller Centre. Bright and fun, Wictoria's work never fails to put a smile on our faces!
